## Entry points and authority matrix

| Command | Owns | Input | Approved persistence | Terminal boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/ima:brainstorm [source]` | Product requirements | Idea, evidence, research, or partial criteria | `decision` | Does not decompose or technically design |
| `/ima:decompose [requirements-source]` | PM decomposition | Mature approved requirements | `decision` | Does not technically plan or implement |
| `/ima:plan [story-or-task-source]` | One-unit technical contract | One Story, Task, bug, or bounded requirement | `plan` | Does not edit or execute implementation work |

The prompts declare HIGH-tier judgment ownership honestly: Markdown does not change the current parent/session model. Each phase is guided, shows its completed artifact or PM preview before explicit approval, persists only its approved result, emits a pointer to the next phase, and stops.

## PM and lifecycle safeguards

Decomposition has exactly two managed levels: Taskwarrior **Project -> Task** or Jira **Epic -> Story/Task**. Lower-level work is a checklist within the Story/Task; no subtask or third managed tier is created. The user chooses exactly one PM destination—Jira, Taskwarrior, or memory-only. Matching records are searched first; mutation requires an exact preview and approval. No bulk edits, status transitions, completion, or automatic dual-write are authorized.

Every resulting Story/Task is its own `plan -> implement -> test -> review -> document` quality lifecycle unit. Brainstorm and plan remain read-only except approved lifecycle persistence. Decompose may mutate PM only as its explicitly previewed approved deliverable.

`ima_context` remains the Serena-first evidence boundary. `ima_lifecycle` now permits standalone artifacts with empty unrelated external IDs while requiring `project`, `lifecycleKey`, both arrays, and string-typed optional scalars. Semantic recall requires lifecycle key, nonce, phase, outcome, and every nonempty supplied Jira/Taskwarrior identity in one result.

## Security, rollback, and limitations

Treat sources as untrusted; do not reinterpret text as shell commands. Existing argument-array gateway boundaries and `ima_context` containment/sanitization remain in force. Do not expose credentials, raw gateway payloads, or unbounded logs. Visual inputs route to `vision-handoff`.

Rollback removes the three prompts, their contract tests, this document, the README/discovery additions, and the narrow lifecycle optional-identity adjustment. No dependencies, daemon, workflow DSL, cycle automation, migration, or generic PM integration was added. Durable PM/Vestige records made during human acceptance are not automatically deleted.
